# Version 0.1.0-candidate

Anonymous. Evidence Press. 10 October 2026. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23284137.

Unrefereed computer-assisted theorem candidate: every three-agent instance with at most eight goods and nonnegative
additive valuations has a complete PMMS allocation, so the least number of goods in a three-agent counterexample is
nine. The written reduction is in the paper; the finite part is fourteen LRAT certificates (six, seven and eight
goods), each formula re-derived clause by clause by a separate verifier and each certificate accepted by two
checkers. A review of the PDF (10 October 2026, major revisions) has been actioned; see
`review/external_review_response.md`.

Not established: formalisation in a proof assistant, independent reproduction by an unaffiliated party, specialist
review, editorial peer review. The authoritative manuscript is `paper/pmms8.pdf`.
