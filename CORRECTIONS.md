# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to pmms-three-agents-eight-goods 0.1.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23284137)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v0.1.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/pmms-three-agents-eight-goods/>.

1. Corrected the example in Observation 3(a). The archived text gives {2, 5}, {1, 4, 6, 7}, {3, 8} as a PMMS allocation of size profile (2, 3, 3); it is a PMMS allocation, but its sizes are (2, 4, 2). A correct example is {1, 2, 5}, {4, 6, 7}, {3, 8}, with goods numbered by column.

   The stated counts (36 PMMS allocations, 28 of profile (2, 3, 3)) are correct and were recomputed. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.

2. Corrected a cross-reference in Observation 3(a). The archived text says the pair template fails for “the instance displayed after Proposition 7”. On that instance the pair template does give PMMS allocations, as Proposition 7 guarantees. The sentence should say that the template “fails for the instance displayed in Remark 8”, where a recomputation finds no PMMS run of the pair template.

   Only the cross-reference is affected. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
